Here in The JVM Lectures, we give detailed lectures for aspiring Civil Engineers relevant to your academic journey. From Algebra up to designing your own structure, we are going to help you understand the fundamentals and principles of the Civil Engineering field.

Here are subjects that we are going to cover:

  1. Pre-calculus
  2. Vectors and Solid Analytic Geometry
  3. Differential and Integral Calculus
  4. Differential Equations
  5. Statistics and Probability
  6. Statics of Rigid Bodies
  7. Dynamics of Rigid Bodies
  8. Strength of Materials of Deformable Bodies
